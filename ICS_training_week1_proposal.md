# ICS Learning Path: Proposal for Feedback (Week 1)

*Draft by Thomas and Sofia. All content below is a proposal for discussion; nothing is built yet.*

## 1. Goal
After the training, a control owner knows **why** we do internal control, **how** to perform and document an assessment in ServiceNow, **what counts as evidence**, what the **quality review** looks at, and what to do when there is an **issue**. The tone should be playful and short, and it should make clear that this is a company expectation, not only a request from the ICS team.

## 2. Proposed structure (follows the ICS cycle)

| # | Chapter | Learning goal | Approx. time | Quiz |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Welcome | Why this matters. Short video from senior management (tone at the top) | 2 min | n/a |
| 1 | Why internal control | Purpose of ICS and why it protects the company and the team | 3 min | 2 questions |
| 2 | The ICS cycle | The four steps: risk assessment, allocation, monitoring, improvement | 4 min | 2 questions |
| 3 | Your tool and your role | Using ServiceNow; executor and reviewer responsibilities | 4 min | 2 questions |
| 4 | Performing an assessment | Testing attributes, sample, **what counts as evidence** (and what does not), scoring | 6 min | 3 scenario questions |
| 5 | The quality review | What is checked, how the score works, good versus weak examples | 4 min | 2 questions |
| 6 | Issues and remediation | When and how to raise an issue, the remediation plan | 4 min | 2 questions |
| 7 | Wrap-up | Where to find help, final quiz | 3 min | 5 questions |

Total: about 30 minutes (to be confirmed).

## 3. Story concept: "Control Town"
- A small town where each district is a step of the cycle (for example the Assessment Office, the Evidence Archive, the Issue Desk).
- The learner is a new resident who helps solve small cases and earns points.
- **Recurring mystery:** "the missing evidence". Each district teaches one clue about what good evidence looks like.
- Characters (placeholders, to be agreed): a friendly guide, a careful reviewer, an executor who says "it's all in the system".
- Same spirit as the corporate security and AI trainings (islands, city, points).

## 4. Key message to reinforce
> A statement is not evidence. "It's in the system" is not proof. Attach what shows the control was performed.
